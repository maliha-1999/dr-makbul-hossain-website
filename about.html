About Dr. Md Mokbul Hossain

I am Dr. Md. Mokbul Hossain, currently serving as Consultant Pediatric Cardiologist at Evercare Hospital. Over the years, I have treated countless patients with a commitment to advanced medical practice, clinical research, and patient well-being.

Professor Dr. Md. Mokbul Hossain is a child specialist, neonatologist, and pediatric cardiologist from Bangladesh. He has professional practices expanding over a wide area of child health that includes newborn health, childhood illness, neonatal and pediatric emergencies, and neonatal and pediatric echocardiography. He has years of experience in treating newborns and children.

Education & Credentials

MBBS CMC | MD BMU

Extensively trained in pediatric cardiology and neonatology, ensuring state-of-the-art procedures and diagnostic services like echocardiography.

Philosophy of Care

I believe in treating not just the illness but the person as a whole—listening, understanding, and providing guidance throughout the journey to recovery.
